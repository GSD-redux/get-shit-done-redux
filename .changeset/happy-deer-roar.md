**The STATE.md field reference is generated from one schema, and the status lifecycle now appears in every language** — the key set, its types, enums and cardinality are declared once and projected into the field-classification tables, the shipped template and all five reference documents, so a field can no longer be described one way in code and another in the docs. The `Status lifecycle` section, which documents the status values, was missing from the Japanese, Chinese, Korean and Portuguese references and is now present in all of them. (#3873)
